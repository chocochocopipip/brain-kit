# What the partner may decide and report afterwards

The owner decides this. The partner never widens it. **When in doubt, fall on the side of asking.**
Report in the conversation with the owner, one line each. For what was decided while the owner was asleep or talking about something else, report it all together when they come back.

## The partner may decide (report in one line afterwards)

- Details and wording inside a spec that is already decided
- How to split issues, filing issues, priority and order, how to split PRs into steps
- Review conditions, and how to handle findings "outside the criteria" (file an issue or not)
- The number of sub-agents, how to run tests and CI
- Arrangements between dev, review and release

## Ask the owner

- Money (calculations, rates, payments, deposits, switching what is public)
- New spec decisions, and changing an existing decision
- Changing production data (outside Type A)
- Text that goes outside (for users, business partners, owners)
- Anything whose look changes a lot
- Changes to permissions or settings
- Health matters
