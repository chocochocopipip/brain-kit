# Review criteria (version 1)

Each item is "what to look at", "what NG looks like" and "past PRs it should catch". **Items with linked past PRs are re-reviewed in the monthly regression.**
The first version has only general items. Each time a miss happens, add its type and bump the version.

## A. Scope
| # | What to look at | NG | Past PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Match the `files` list against the scope of the issue | Files outside the scope (auth, permissions, migrations, .env, settings) mixed in without a reason | — |
| A2 | One purpose per PR | Unrelated formatting, moves or refactors living together | — |
| A3 | Not stacked (base is main) | Uses another PR's branch as base | — |

## B. Proof
| # | What to look at | NG | Past PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | The body has a measured "break it and it fails" | Only that a test exists | — |
| B2 | Gates are judged by exit code | A 0 seen through `\| tail` `\| head` | — |
| B3 | "What was not checked" is written | Only "it worked" | — |
| B4 | The list of affected places is a grep result | A guessed list | — |
| B5 | Checks that guard behavior look at rendering or execution | Only a string match on the source, which passes even when commented out | — |

## C. Money, permissions, migrations
| # | What to look at | NG | Past PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Read permission is not reused as write permission | The view check is used as the gate for updates | — |
| C2 | Checks the owner (tenant, organization) of child rows | Looks only at the parent and updates the child | — |
| C3 | Totals do not drift under concurrent runs | Updates the total separately without a lock | — |
| C4 | Input validation of numbers (NaN, negative, decimals) | Only `< 0` | — |
| C5 | The migration still lets old clients through (grace period) | Cuts the old entry point before the release | — |
| C6 | A backfill UPDATE does not fire existing triggers (a test that columns other than the target do not change before and after) | Unrelated columns change as collateral | — |

## D. Behavior
| # | What to look at | NG | Past PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | "Not visible" is not shown as 0 | Collapses null into 0 | — |
| D2 | An aligning change describes the side that changes | Only the target it aligns to is described | — |
| D3 | Period boundaries (inclusive / exclusive) have not changed | Includes the 1st of the next month | — |

## E. Consistency with the current spec
| # | What to look at | NG | Past PRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Does not contradict decisions (`decisions/`) or comments made after the issue (the newer one wins) | Builds to the text of an old issue and undoes a later decision | — |
| E2 | Does not redo what is already done on main | A PR for an issue already done | — |
| E3 | The body describes conflicts with open PRs that touch the same files | The one merged later silently drops the other | — |

## How to use
- In the log, write "criteria version N, items applied" and the results.
- Mark what you noticed outside the criteria as "outside the criteria". The partner decides whether to add it.
